# **1. Executive Summary** 

AYOT SafeSport™ is an integrated **sports health, injury prevention, and athlete performance platform** designed for schools, sports academies, clubs, and tournament organizers. 

The platform combines **AI-powered movement risk screening, sports medical coverage, digital athlete health records, and injury prevention programs** to provide structured medical oversight for youth sports. 

Across Kenya, millions of students participate in sports each year, yet most institutions lack access to sports medicine services, athlete screening, and injury monitoring systems. This leads to preventable injuries, delayed recovery, and growing institutional liability risks. 

AYOT SafeSport™ addresses this gap through a **scalable hybrid model combining digital technology and mobile sports medicine services** . 

Key features include: 

- AI-assisted movement and injury-risk screening 

- Mobile sports medicine support during games and training 

- Digital athlete health records and incident reporting 

- Injury prevention and rehabilitation programs 

- Institutional sports safety and compliance systems 

The platform is designed to be **affordable, scalable, and deployable across Kenya’s education and sports ecosystem.** 

AYOT SafeSport™ aims to become the **national infrastructure platform for athlete health and safety in youth sports.** 

# **2. Problem Statement** 

Sports participation among young people in Kenya is rapidly increasing due to school programs, youth academies, and national sports initiatives. 

However, the sports ecosystem faces significant gaps: 

### **Lack of Sports Medical Support** 

Most schools and clubs operate sports programs without professional medical oversight. 

### **High Rates of Preventable Injuries** 

Non-contact injuries such as ankle sprains, ACL injuries, and muscle strains are common but often preventable. 

### **No Athlete Health Monitoring** 

Schools rarely maintain structured records of athlete injuries, recovery progress, or return-to-play clearance. 

### **Limited Access to Sports Science** 

Advanced injury risk screening and performance analysis are available only to elite athletes or professional teams. 

### **Institutional Risk Exposure** 

Schools and tournament organizers face increasing legal and reputational risks when injuries occur without proper documentation or medical oversight. 

These challenges highlight the urgent need for a **structured, scalable sports health system for youth athletes.** 

# **3. Solution** 

AYOT SafeSport™ provides an integrated platform that enables schools and sports organizations to operate safer and more professional sports programs. 

The platform integrates four core components. 

## **3.1 AI Movement Risk Screening** 

Athletes perform standardized movement drills that are recorded using smartphone video. 

AI-based pose estimation analyzes biomechanical movement patterns to identify injury risk factors such as: 

- Knee valgus during landing 

- Poor trunk stability 

- Limb asymmetry 

- Delayed movement stabilization 

Each athlete receives a **personalized injury risk report** with corrective guidance. 

Teams receive **risk dashboards** that help coaches identify injury patterns within their squads.

Output:

Example: 

```
{

"left_shoulder": (x,y),
"right_shoulder": (x,y),
"left_knee": (x,y),
"right_knee": (x,y)

```

## **4.2 Biomechanical Metrics** 

Metrics calculated: 

### **Knee Valgus Angle** 

Detects knee inward collapse during landing. 

Thresholds 

Low Risk: <5° Moderate Risk: 5–10° High Risk: >10° 

### **Trunk Lean Angle** 

Measures torso stability. 

Low Risk: <10° Moderate Risk: 10–20° High Risk: >20° 

### **Limb Symmetry Index** 

Measures bilateral performance. 

```
LSI = weaker limb / stronger limb × 100
```

Low Risk: ≥90% Moderate Risk: 80–89% High Risk: <80% 

### **Time to Stabilization** 

Measures landing control. 

Low Risk: <0.5 seconds Moderate Risk: 0.5–1.0 seconds High Risk: >1.0 seconds 

## **4.3 Composite Risk Score** 

Each metric assigned: 

Green = 0 Amber = 1 Red = 2 

Total Risk Score: 

#### **Score Classification** 

- 0–2 Low Risk 

- 3–5 Moderate Risk 

- 6+ High Risk

# **4. Feature Engineering** 

Raw pose estimation coordinates are transformed into **biomechanical metrics** . 

Example: 

### **Knee Valgus Angle** 

Computed using joint coordinates. 

\theta = \cos^{-1}\left( \frac{(hip-knee) \cdot (ankle-knee)}{|hip-knee| |ankle-knee|} \right) 

This measures the angle between hip–knee and ankle–knee vectors. 

### **Limb Symmetry Index** 

LSI = \frac{weaker\ limb}{stronger\ limb} \times 100 

Used to detect strength imbalance. 

### **Stabilization Time** 

Measured from landing frame until center-of-mass oscillation stabilizes.
